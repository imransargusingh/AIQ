Design note: the 51 tasks still without a job

Cloud-only, started under Imran's standing rule. Built from the repo only: the design notes no-job-tasks and run-or-ship-pack, and the judge code in crates/aiq/src/judge.rs. I could not read the laptop store, so every number below is either quoted from a measured laptop run (named) or worked out from those runs (marked).

Outcome

Recommendation: leave the 51 as they are. Build no fifth pack and no catch-all. One survey and one check on the laptop confirm or overturn this. Neither needs Imran's time beyond the premium requests.

  • A catch-all already exists in effect. A task with no job is unclassified: Home shows it as

"Unclassified" (apps/home/src/format.ts) and no tip can fire for it. A named catch-all pack with no tips would change a label, not anyone's habits.

  • A fifth pack would need one kind of work big enough to coach and to check. The numbers below say no such

kind exists.

Why no fifth pack (the bar)

A pack's tips stay on only if a different-model check agrees on its job 8 of 10, with at least 10 checked (rung-model US-7, judge-accuracy-check K6b). A pack with fewer than 10 tasks can never pass that check, so its tips would never be proven. Tips also need a kind repeated 3 times in 30 days.

So the bar for a fifth pack: one coachable kind (not chat_or_test, not other) on at least 10 of the 31 C1 tasks. On the survey proportions the largest kind would be about 3 of 31. That is far below the bar.

Why no new pack for C2 and C3 (20 tasks)

These are not outside the packs. The judge named a pack job but was not sure. The fix, if any, is rule R2 (both runs agree on a pack job at 0.5 or more), already built and measured but not live (no-job-tasks N2). R2 would give a job to 15 of the 51 (measured, run-or-ship-pack). None of those 15 has been checked since run_ship; the earlier 4 of 7 was on a different set. R2 goes live only at 8 of 10 with at least 10 checked (N3).

Laptop readings that settle it

Both are measurements only. Neither changes a judgment, a tip or a setting.

1. Survey all C1 tasks (decides the fifth-pack question). Stores nothing; prints counts and generic labels. About 31 premium requests.

aiq judge --survey-other --sample 31
  • If one coachable kind reaches 10 of 31: draft a fifth pack for it and ask Imran.
  • If operate_systems or set_up_tools reach 4 or more: run_ship is missing tasks. The fix is the run_ship

wording in the judge prompt (a judge version bump), not a new pack.

  • Otherwise: leave C1 as it is. This note becomes accepted.

2. Check every no-job task (decides R2 for C2 and C3). Stores the check answers, like every check run. About 51 premium requests, outside the judging budget. A sample of 30 would give R2 only about 9 checks (5 per cause first, then random), short of the 10 the bar needs, so take all 51.

aiq judge --check --no-job --sample 51
aiq judge --check-report
  • In the report, read the line R2 (...): gives a job to N of 51; second reading agreed k of n.
  • If R2 agrees at least 8 of 10 with at least 10 checked: propose switching R2 on (Imran's card).
  • If not: R2 stays off, and these 20 stay uncoached (about 4% of tasks).
