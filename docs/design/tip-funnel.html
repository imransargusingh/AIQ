The tip funnel

Why

Imran (2026-11-08): "It's super important that AIQ offers tips when it is supposed to." From 11-04 16:00Z to 11-08 the label self-check held every label tip and the words check had nothing to match, so no tip was decided on any surface for four days. The only trace was one log line per core run. A stop that silent is a defect class: a hold that is invisible to the person and to us. The control is a count that cannot be missed.

Data model

Grain: one row is exactly one point where a live new task's next-step tip path ended or began to wait (tip_path, store v11, append-only, no text). The latest row per task is its state. A task waiting on a kind label has label_asked, then the label's end. Everything after a tip is decided is already stored: card_decision (held, ready, expired), card_delivery (shown, by tool), card_open, tip_feedback.

Steps (aiq_domain::funnel::Step), each written at exactly one exit of the core's path:

decideddecide_kindA tip was decided (see its card decision)
kind_too_few · kind_right_rung · kind_blocked · kind_not_sure · kind_flaggeddecide_kindKind known, its tip held on purpose by the kind's state (D6)

Measures: every count is additive over tasks or cards. Periods: since local midnight, and the last 7 days. One reader (aiq_core::funnel::tip_funnels) feeds aiq status and Home; one place holds the words (aiq_domain::funnel::words).

Dry day

A local day before today, in the last 7, with 3 or more new tasks and no tip shown. assume: 3 tasks is a day of work (not measured). If it warns on light days or misses dry ones, change DROUGHT_MIN_TASKS.

Surfaces

  • aiq status: the dry days first, then the week (tasks, kind known, tip found, tips, shown, opened, answered), why

tasks had no tip, why tips were held, expired, waiting, shown per tool; then today's line.

  • Home: a closable note for the newest dry day ("No tips yesterday across 6 tasks. Main reason: …", See why).

Closed stays closed for that day.

  • Habits and tips: "Tips this week": one sentence, five bars (new tasks, kind known, tip ready, shown, opened) with

the step that lost the most marked, why tasks had no tip, and why ready tips were held.

Tests (the controls)

  • Domain: every step and every hold reason has words; each step counts under its own code; latest step wins; a dry

day is found, named by its main reason, and cleared by a shown tip, a light day, today, or age.

  • Core: every exit of the path writes its step end to end (next_rung.rs, service.rs); status carries the

funnel and a dry day.

  • CLI: the status text leads with the dry day and uses no "judge".
  • Home: unit tests of the sentence, the marked step and the day words; e2e of the card, the note, See why, close,

and axe in light and dark.
